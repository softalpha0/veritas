How it works

From a contract address to a verdict in about a minute.

  1. 01

    Pull the holders

    Veritas loads the top 200 holders from Nansen, tagged with Smart Money, exchange and fund labels, and each wallet's 24h, 7d and 30d balance changes.

  2. 02

    Link the wallets

    For the largest real owners it asks Nansen who funded them and who they're linked to. Wallets sharing a first funder or multisig collapse into one owner.

  3. 03

    Score and explain

    Concentration, hidden clusters, insider selling, Smart Money conviction and fresh-wallet demand combine into a 0–100 score where every point is explained.
